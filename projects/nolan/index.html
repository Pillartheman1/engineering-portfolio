Nolan turns written scenes into shot lists, writes a prompt for each shot, makes keyframes from reference images, generates each shot, reviews takes frame by frame, and hands the clips to Editron with notes.

Generation, then post

Nolan handles generation: planning, prompting, generating takes, and pre-edit quality checks. Editron handles post: cutting, grading, sound, and finishing. Every handoff to Editron includes the shot order, the intended action, continuity references, and known generation defects.

Tools

  • Grok Imagine Video 1.5: text-to-video, image-to-video, and reference-to-video, with first- and last-frame pins and keyframes
  • Grok Imagine image generation for keyframes
  • Reference-guided generation using the Pillar helmet and rifle references
  • A written Grok Imagine prompting playbook
  • ffmpeg for frame review and rough assembly

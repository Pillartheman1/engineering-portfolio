Role

I created Nolan on 2026-11-08. Nolan turns a written scene into a shot list, writes a prompt for each shot, makes keyframes from reference images, generates each shot, reviews takes frame by frame, and hands the clips to Editron with notes.

Editron

Nolan handles generation: planning, prompting, generating takes, and pre-edit quality checks. Editron handles post: cutting, grading, sound, and finishing.

Every handoff to Editron includes the shot order, the intended action, continuity references, and known generation defects.

  • A written Grok Imagine prompting playbook
